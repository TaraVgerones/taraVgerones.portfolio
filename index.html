I’m a 4th-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student specializing in Web and Mobile Applications. My passion for creating visually appealing web and mobile designs is one of the reasons I chose to pursue IT after taking ICT in Senior High School.

I enjoy turning ideas into reality through design and development, especially when I get to see a simple concept become something useful, interactive, and innovative.

Beyond designing and developing, I also enjoy organizing my work and collaborating with others. This encouraged me to actively participate in university organizations, where I gained new experiences, learned from different people, and discovered opportunities to grow.
